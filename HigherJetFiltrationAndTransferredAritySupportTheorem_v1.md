# Higher-Jet Invariant Space, Transferred Arity Support, and Nontruncation Theorem

**Date:** 2026-08-28

**Status:** exact finite theorem on the selected 48-dimensional q79 response retract

**Claim boundary:** this is a theorem about the finite transferred `A_infinity`
structure. It is not an identification with `D_fin`, a continuum HYM vertex, an
observed interaction, or a selected physical action.

## 1. Purpose

The complete arity-four computation established that the transferred operation
`m4` is nonzero, but it left two distinct questions:

1. Is there a structural rule controlling how the twelve-dimensional
   higher-jet harmonic ideal `J` can enter operations of arbitrary arity?
2. Does the transferred hierarchy stop at any finite arity?

A direct `m5` table starts with

```text
48^5 = 254,803,968
```

basis quintuples, so a structural reduction should precede brute force. The
result below supplies that reduction and, independently, an exact nonzero `m5`
witness.

## 2. Exact setup

Let `V` be the 144-dimensional symmetric signed Weyl DGA and let

```text
T = C direct-sum J
```

be its 48-dimensional strong deformation retract. Here `C` is the selected old
q79 cochain complex and `J` is the zero-mode harmonic ideal generated by the
orientation-even signed-edge plane. Write

```text
i : T -> V,
p : V -> T,
H : V -> V[-1]
```

for the already certified inclusion, projection, and transfer homotopy. They
satisfy

```text
p i = 1,
d H + H d = 1 - i p,
H^2 = 0,
p H = 0,
H i = 0.
```

The source product is denoted by `mu`. Fourier modes are elements
`g=(a,b)` of `Z3 x Z3`.

## 3. Why a fixed parity count is insufficient

At zero mode, `C` is the exterior algebra of the orientation-odd plane and `J`
contains every harmonic form with at least one orientation-even factor. Away
from zero mode, however, the exact old inclusion is phase-twisted:

```text
I_g(c_x,c_z)
  = (c_x, -omega^a c_x, c_z, -omega^(-b) c_z).
```

Consequently a fixed even-factor count is not preserved by the noncommutative
mode product. The correct invariant object is not a guessed global filtration;
it is the image of the exact homotopy in each nonzero mode.

## 4. The six-dimensional homotopy image

For every nonzero mode `g`, define

```text
R_g = image(H restricted to V_g).
```

Exact row reduction over

```text
Q(omega),  omega^2 + omega + 1 = 0
```

gives

```text
dim R_g = 6
```

for all eight nonzero modes, with graded dimensions

```text
degree 1: 2
degree 2: 3
degree 3: 1.
```

The same space is generated by the first one-old/one-jet excursions:

```text
H mu(iC_g, iJ)  and  H mu(iJ, iC_g).
```

Thus this is not merely an upper bound on reachable states. It is the complete
nonzero-mode homotopy image relevant to a transfer tree after its first mixed
edge.

## 5. Exact invariant-space lemma

### Lemma 5.1

For every nonzero mode `g` and every `j in J`,

```text
H mu(R_g, i j) is contained in R_g,
H mu(i j, R_g) is contained in R_g,
p mu(R_g, i j) = 0,
p mu(i j, R_g) = 0.
```

Moreover,

```text
H mu(iJ, iJ) = 0.
```

### Certificate

The verifier constructs `R_g` independently in two ways:

1. by spanning all first mixed excursions and closing under all left and right
   `J` operators;
2. by spanning `H` applied to all sixteen exterior basis vectors in mode `g`.

The two row-reduced spaces agree exactly in every mode. For each mode, all

```text
6 x 12 x 2 = 144
```

invariance images and all 144 terminal projections are checked. Across eight
modes this is 1,152 exact tests of each kind. Every residual is zero. All 144
pure-`J` basis-pair homotopies also vanish.

The packet records a separate SHA-256 digest of the canonical row-reduced basis
for each mode. The independent verifier reconstructs and matches all eight
digests.

## 6. General higher-jet support theorem

### Theorem 6.1

For every `n >= 3`, the transferred operation `m_n` vanishes whenever at least
`n-1` inputs lie in `J`.

Equivalently, a nonzero `m_n` value can contain at most `n-2` higher-jet inputs.

### Proof

Expand `m_n` as the signed sum of planar binary transfer trees. Every internal
edge except the root edge carries `H`.

First suppose all inputs lie in `J`. Every tree with at least three leaves has
a non-root cherry consisting of two `J` leaves. Its outgoing edge is

```text
H mu(iJ, iJ) = 0,
```

so that tree vanishes.

Now suppose exactly one input lies in `C`. If it is a zero-mode old input, the
whole tree remains at zero mode and its first non-root `H` edge vanishes. Assume
therefore that it has nonzero mode `g`.

Any non-root subtree that does not contain the unique `C` input is a pure-`J`
subtree and vanishes by the previous argument. Hence every surviving non-root
subtree must contain the distinguished `C` leaf. A planar binary tree with this
property is necessarily a comb along that leaf: at each stage its sibling is a
single `J` leaf.

The first mixed edge is in `R_g`. Lemma 5.1 keeps every subsequent comb edge in
`R_g`. At the root, the last `J` leaf is attached without a following homotopy,
but Lemma 5.1 gives

```text
p mu(R_g, iJ) = p mu(iJ, R_g) = 0.
```

Thus every planar tree vanishes separately. The conclusion is independent of
the Koszul signs and holds for all `n >= 3`. QED.

### Corollary 6.2

The previously observed support rules are instances of one theorem:

```text
m3: at most one J input,
m4: at most two J inputs,
m5: at most three J inputs,
...
```

The theorem does not say that every permitted sector is nonzero.

## 7. Exact m5 formula and witness

The sign convention is the one already calibrated against the complete `m3`
and `m4` packets. Merkulov's displayed arity-five recursion becomes

```text
lambda5(a,b,c,d,e)
  =  mu(H lambda4(a,b,c,d), i e)
   + (-1)^(|a|+|b|+|c|) mu(H lambda3(a,b,c), H lambda2(d,e))
   - mu(H lambda2(a,b), H lambda3(c,d,e))
   - (-1)^|a| mu(i a, H lambda4(b,c,d,e)),

m5 = p lambda5.
```

An exact deterministic probe over 86,796 degree-admissible quintuples gives the
witness

```text
m5(
  C:0,0,1,
  C:0,0,1,
  C:0,0,1,
  C:1,0,1,
  C:1,0,0
)
  = (1/24 + omega/48) C:2,0,1.
```

The input degree is four, so the output degree is one, exactly as required by
`|m5|=-3`. A second implementation reconstructs the complete `lambda2` through
`lambda5` expression and reproduces this value without importing the builder.
Therefore the transferred hierarchy does not truncate at `m4`.

This witness proves nonvanishing only. It is not a replacement for the complete
`m5` operation table or the arity-five Stasheff identity.

## 8. All-arity nontruncation theorem

The `m5` witness belongs to a family with a closed exact recurrence. Set

```text
x = C:0,0,1,
y = C:1,0,1,
z = C:1,0,0,
M_k = m_(k+2)(x^k,y,z).
```

Use Merkulov's formal convention `H lambda1=-i` and define the source states

```text
A_k = H lambda_(k+2)(x^k,y,z),
B_k = H lambda_(k+1)(x^k,y),
T(v) = H mu(i x,v),
U(v) = H mu(v,i z).
```

Because `x` is zero-mode harmonic, every `H lambda_r(x^r)` with `r>=2`
vanishes. In the all-arity Merkulov recursion this deletes every interior split
of the words above. The two surviving edge splits give

```text
B_k = (-1)^k T B_(k-1),
A_k = (-1)^(k+1) (T A_(k-1) + U B_k),
M_k = (-1)^(k+1) p(mu(i x,A_(k-1)) + mu(B_k,i z)).
```

The state update is linear and depends only on the parity of `k`. Exact
arithmetic over `Q(omega)` gives the two parity bases

```text
(A_3,B_3) = (A_1,B_1)/12,
(A_4,B_4) = (A_2,B_2)/12.
```

Applying the same two parity-dependent update maps proves inductively that

```text
(A_(k+2),B_(k+2)) = (A_k,B_k)/12   for every k>=1.
```

The independently computed output bases are

```text
M_1 = (2+omega)/4 C:2,0,1,
M_2 = -omega/8 C:2,0,1,
M_3 = M_1/12,
M_4 = M_2/12.
```

The state recurrence then gives `M_(k+2)=M_k/12` for every later `k`. Therefore:

### Theorem 8.1

For every integer `r>=0`,

```text
m_(2r+3)(x^(2r+1),y,z)
  = (2+omega)/(4*12^r) C:2,0,1,

m_(2r+4)(x^(2r+2),y,z)
  = -omega/(8*12^r) C:2,0,1.
```

Both coefficients are nonzero in `Q(omega)`. Hence the transferred structure
has a nonzero `m_n` for every `n>=3`; it does not truncate at any finite arity.
QED.

The executable certificate also evaluates the family directly through arity

induction.

This theorem concerns one ordered input family. It does not provide complete
operation tables or Stasheff residual tables from arity five onward.
