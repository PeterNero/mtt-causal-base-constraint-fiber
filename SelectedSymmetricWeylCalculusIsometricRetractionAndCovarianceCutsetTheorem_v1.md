# Selected Symmetric Weyl Calculus, Isometric Retraction and Covariance Cutset Theorem v1

## 1. Purpose

The selected two-direction Weyl-Koszul complex has the correct finite Hodge
shadow, but its forward-only multiplication is not covariant under reflection
or the Fourier quarter-turn.  The preceding theorem measured those product
defects exactly.

This theorem constructs the minimal forward/backward completion, computes its
full Hodge theory, and determines whether it can replace the selected complex
without changing the physical content.  The answer is two-sided:

- the symmetric completion removes every finite product-covariance defect;
- it introduces additional harmonic classes and therefore cannot be promoted
  as the same physical complex without another selection theorem.

No observed value, fitted coefficient or new physical selector is used.

## 2. Minimal signed-direction orbit

Begin with the selected positive directions `+x,+z`.  Reflection requires
`-x,-z`, while Fourier rotation acts by

```text
+x -> +z -> -x -> -z -> +x.
```

Hence the smallest direction set closed under both operations is

\[
 S=\{+x,-x,+z,-z\}.
\]

The executable certificate checks all proper subsets containing `+x,+z`; none
is closed.  The four-direction completion is therefore minimal for these
symmetries.

\[

\[

## 4. Exact finite covariance

Affine `S3` acts on coefficients by the selected q79 affine conjugations.
Translations preserve the four direction labels and reflection exchanges each
opposite pair.  Fourier acts on coefficients by

\[
 FZF^{-1}=X^{-1},\qquad FXF^{-1}=Z
\]

and cyclically permutes the four signed forms.

These actions are exact DGA automorphisms.  Thus the previous reflection and
Fourier product defects become zero on the symmetric calculus.

The group generated on the finite coefficient/direction system is

\[
 G_{36}=(\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_3)\rtimes C_4,
 \qquad |G_{36}|=36.
\]

The selected affine `S3` is its order-six subgroup generated by one Weyl
translation and the square of Fourier.

This is an exact covariance group, not a new selected physical holonomy group.
In particular full-chain Fourier still does not centralize translated `S3`
actions.  The q79 branch therefore cannot be silently retyped from `S3`
holonomy to `G36` holonomy.

## 6. Exact selected-complex retraction

For a simultaneous Weyl mode, define the degree-one inclusion

\[
 I(c_x,c_z)=
 (c_x,-\alpha^{-1}c_x,c_z,-\beta^{-1}c_z)
\]

and retraction

\[
 Q(b_+,b_-,c_+,c_-)
 =\left(\frac{b_+-\alpha b_-}{2},
         \frac{c_+-\beta c_-}{2}\right).
\]

In higher degrees use exterior powers, with `Q=0` above old degree two.  Exact
mode arithmetic gives

\[
 QI=1,
 \qquad d_{\rm sym}I=Id_{\rm old},
 \qquad Qd_{\rm sym}=d_{\rm old}Q.
\]

For the half-edge metric, `I` is isometric and `Q=I^*`.  Therefore

\[
 R=IQ
\]

is an orthogonal cochain projector.  Its image is a reducing summand for the
Hodge Laplacian and reproduces the selected old spectrum, Green and homotopy.

On harmonic one-forms its image is precisely

\[
 \operatorname{span}\{\theta_{+x}-\theta_{-x},
                       \theta_{+z}-\theta_{-z}\}.
\]

Reflection acts by `-I2`, Fourier acts by

\[
 j=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]

and the exterior algebra of this plane has dimensions `(1,2,1)`.  This is
exactly the previously selected q79 harmonic strain shadow.

## 7. Extra harmonic modes

The symmetric cohomology is larger than the selected image.  Degree by degree,
the complementary harmonic dimensions are

\[

\]

These classes are harmonic, not exact.  Consequently `Q,I` define a split
isometric cochain retract but not a quasi-isomorphism and not a Hodge
contraction of the whole symmetric complex onto the selected complex.  The
additional classes cannot be declared gauge, auxiliary or unphysical without
a new selected rule.

## 8. Product verdict

The selected harmonic image is an exact exterior subalgebra, and its sixteen
basis-pair products agree with the old harmonic product with zero leakage.

Off harmonics, the result is different.  Coefficient twisting mixes the
orientation-odd and orientation-even signed forms.  Thus the selected
cochain image is not a subalgebra of the symmetric DGA.  Orthogonal
compression

\[
 \mu_R(x,y)=Q\bigl(Ix\cdot_{\rm sym}Iy\bigr)
\]

does not reproduce the old forward product on every basis pair and is not
associative on every basis triple.  Exhaustive exact evaluation gives

```text
selected-image product leakage:       864 / 1296 basis pairs
compressed-versus-old product defect: 504 / 1296 basis pairs
nonzero compressed associators:      4464 / 46656 basis triples
harmonic product defects:                0 / 16 basis pairs
harmonic image leakage:                  0 / 16 basis pairs
```

This is the decisive cutset.  Full finite covariance, the old selected
cochain/Hodge object and an associative selected full-chain product cannot all
be identified by this simple orthogonal retraction.  A transferred
`A_infinity` product or the actual continuum HYM deformation algebra would be
a new construction.

## 9. Meaning for the preprojection program

The symmetric calculus is a serious upper reference-system candidate:

```text
four signed Weyl directions with exact G36 covariance
    -> canonical orientation-odd Hodge summand
    -> selected 1,2,1 harmonic exterior algebra
```

This advances the mathematical language program but does not close the
physical q79 geometry.  A physical theorem must either select the
