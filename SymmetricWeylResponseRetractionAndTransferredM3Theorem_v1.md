# Symmetric Weyl Response Retraction and Transferred m3 Theorem v1

## 1. Purpose

The symmetric four-direction Weyl calculus solved finite covariance but was
too large to identify with the selected two-direction q79 response complex.
The old complex was an exact cochain summand, yet it discarded twelve genuine
harmonic classes and its compressed binary product was nonassociative.

The first-jet theorem subsequently identified those twelve classes as the
higher-jet harmonic ideal `J`. This theorem retains both pieces. It proves an
exact strong deformation retract

\[
 \Omega_{\rm sym}\quad\rightleftarrows\quad
 T=\Omega_{\rm old}\oplus J
\]

and transfers the symmetric DGA product to `T` through arity three.

The result is decisive at its declared tier:

- the correct finite target has dimension `48`, not `36`;
- its binary product remains nonassociative off harmonics;
- a nonzero ternary product `m3` supplies the exact arity-three homotopy
  correction;
- the old associator is therefore transfer data, not evidence that no coherent
  product exists.

No continuum HYM endpoint, `D_fin` identification or physical action is
inferred.

## 4. Exact strong deformation retract

Let `I:C->A` and `Q:A->C` be the previously proved isometric cochain inclusion
and retraction. Let `P_J` be the orthogonal zero-mode projector onto `J`. Define

\[
 i_T(x,j)=I(x)+j,
\]

\[
 p_T(y)=(Qy,P_Jy).
\]

Write

\[
 K=1-IQ.
\]

On nonzero Weyl modes, let

\[
 h_{\rm sym}=d_{\rm sym}^*G_{\rm sym};
\]

on the zero mode set `h_sym=0`. The required transfer homotopy is

\[
 H_T=K h_{\rm sym}K.
 \tag{1}
\]

### Theorem 4.1

The maps `(i_T,p_T,H_T)` form a strong deformation retract:

\[
 p_Ti_T=1,
\]

\[
 d_AH_T+H_Td_A=1-i_Tp_T,
 \tag{2}
\]

\[
 H_T^2=0,\qquad p_TH_T=0,\qquad H_Ti_T=0.
 \tag{3}
\]

### Proof

The old image is a reducing Hodge summand, so `IQ` commutes with `d_A`, the
Laplacian, the Green operator and `h_sym`. Hence `K` does as well. The symmetric
Hodge contraction gives

\[
 d_Ah_{\rm sym}+h_{\rm sym}d_A=1-P_H,
\]

where `P_H` is the full sixteen-dimensional harmonic projector. Its old
component is the orientation-odd harmonic projector and its `K` component is
exactly `P_J`. Therefore

\[
 d_AH_T+H_Td_A
 =K(1-P_H)K
 =K-P_J
 =1-IQ-P_J
 =1-i_Tp_T.
\]

The Hodge side conditions and orthogonality of the old and `J` images give
(3). Direct exact arithmetic verifies (2) on all 144 source basis elements and
`p_Ti_T=1` on all 48 target basis elements. QED.

## 5. Transferred products

Let `mu` be the associative product on `A`. Homological transfer defines

\[
 m_1=d_T,
\]

\[
 m_2(a,b)=p_T\mu(i_Ta,i_Tb),
 \tag{4}
\]

and, with the cohomological sign convention used by the executable packet,

\[
 \begin{split}
 m_3(a,b,c)={}&p_T\mu(H_T\mu(i_Ta,i_Tb),i_Tc)\\
 &-(-1)^{|a|}p_T\mu(i_Ta,H_T\mu(i_Tb,i_Tc)).
 \end{split}
 \tag{5}
\]

The arity-three Stasheff identity is

\[
 \begin{split}
 0={}&m_2(m_2(a,b),c)-m_2(a,m_2(b,c))+m_1m_3(a,b,c)\\
 &+m_3(m_1a,b,c)+(-1)^{|a|}m_3(a,m_1b,c)\\
 &+(-1)^{|a|+|b|}m_3(a,b,m_1c).
 \end{split}
 \tag{6}
\]

Equation (6) says exactly how `m3` repairs the binary associator up to the
target differential. It does not say that `m2` becomes strictly associative.

## 7. Concrete coupled witness

The packet labels old basis elements by `C:a,b,mask` and ideal elements by
`J:mask`. For

```text
a = C:0,0,1,
b = C:1,0,1,
c = C:2,0,0,
```

both the binary associator and `m3` are nonzero. Writing each coefficient as
`u+v*omega`, the associator has the ideal component

```text

```

while `m3` has

```text
J:4       -> -1/4 - (1/4) omega,
C:0,0,1   ->  1/4 - (1/4) omega.
```

The differential terms in (6) cancel the associator exactly. This is an
explicit witness that the higher-jet ideal participates in the chain-level
repair rather than merely padding the cohomology count.

## 8. Relation to the previous 4,464 associators

The earlier number `4,464` counted associators after projecting every product
back to the old 36-dimensional complex. The new 48-dimensional target retains
ideal-valued binary outputs, so its old-input associator count is `4,996` and
its full count is `7,124`. These counts are not expected to agree: they concern
different codomains.

The important invariant statement is equation (6), not a decrease in the raw
associator count. The old compression failed because it requested a strict
binary algebra after deleting both acyclic excursions and higher-jet harmonic
outputs. The strong deformation retract shows that the correct finite object
is an `A_infinity` algebra whose first new operation is nonzero `m3`.

## 9. Physical and continuum meaning

This result closes the finite low-arity transfer placeholder. It gives a
mathematically coherent preprojection reference system in which:

```text
exact symmetric covariance upstairs
    -> old q79 nonzero-mode response lanes plus higher-jet harmonics
    -> strict harmonic first-jet quotient when required
    -> nonzero m3 correcting off-harmonic binary compression.
```

It does not prove that this finite `m3` is a physical interaction vertex. Such
a claim requires the selected HYM endpoint, its connection and Green operator,
an intertwiner from this finite target to the physical deformation complex,
and compatibility with the selected action. The existing repair/action cutset
also forbids identifying a positive Hodge transfer coefficient with a signed
physical vertex without that bridge.
